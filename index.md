# Privacy Policy for Cipher Launcher

Effective date — 17 November 2026

Cipher Launcher is an Android launcher developed by Zahid Thoker. This policy describes the information processed by version 0.16.2, including its optional screen-time, Focus, screen-lock and experimental reel-counter features.

## Information processed on your device

Cipher processes installed launchable app names, package identifiers and icons to display, search, launch and organise your apps. Your layout, folders, favourites, preferences and Focus selections are stored in the app's private local storage.

With Usage Access enabled, Cipher reads Android app-usage and screen-state events to calculate screen time and time spent in supported social apps. These calculations run on your device.

Cipher reads the battery level and charging state to display its battery indicator.

## Optional Accessibility services

You choose whether to enable each service in Android settings.

- The experimental reel counter examines accessibility events and visible interface elements in YouTube, Instagram and Facebook to detect Shorts or Reels. It can process app identifiers, interface labels, captions or creator text when exposed by those apps, and scroll positions or distances. Temporary identifiers, including hashes derived from available video text, are held in memory to recognise changes. Cipher stores daily per-app counts locally. Counts are estimates and depend on the information exposed by each social app.
- Focus uses the foreground app's package identifier to recognise apps you selected for blocking. It displays a blocking overlay during your chosen session and can return you to the home screen. Its service is configured without access to window contents.
- Double-tap lock uses Android's Accessibility screen-lock action when requested. Its service is configured without access to window contents.

These features process information locally. Cipher does not transmit accessibility data to the developer or third parties, and does not save copies of video captions, creator text or screen contents to persistent storage.

## Other permissions

Display-over-other-apps access lets Cipher show its floating time and counter bar. Foreground-service and notification permissions support that user-enabled feature and its ongoing notification. The uninstall permission lets Cipher request Android's normal app-uninstallation confirmation when you choose to uninstall an app.

The phone shortcut opens your device's dialler. This version does not request contacts, call-log, microphone, camera or location access.

## Transmission and sharing

This version has no Internet permission, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Cipher does not send your app inventory, usage activity, accessibility data or local settings to a server, sell that information, or share it with advertisers.

If you contact the developer by email, the email provider processes that message. The developer receives the address and information you choose to include and uses it to respond and troubleshoot your request. Support correspondence may be retained while needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You may request its deletion by email, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Google Play, Android, your device manufacturer and other apps operate under their own privacy policies. This policy covers Cipher's handling of information.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Launcher settings and Focus configuration remain in Cipher's private local storage until you change them, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Daily reel counts are replaced when the first new count is recorded on a later day. Temporary detection data is held in memory during operation. Android maintains the underlying usage history according to its own system behaviour.

To delete Cipher's local stored data, open Android Settings, select Cipher Launcher under Apps, open Storage and clear its data, or uninstall Cipher. Clearing data resets your launcher preferences and folders. Cipher disables Android application backup through its app configuration. Device-to-device transfer behaviour may depend on your device manufacturer.

You can revoke Usage Access, Accessibility, overlay and notification permissions in Android settings. Features that depend on those permissions will stop working or become unavailable.

## Security

Cipher uses Android's private app storage and permission controls to limit access to its local data. The security of your device also depends on your Android version, device settings and installed software.

## Changes to this policy

The policy will be updated when Cipher's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current revision. Features introducing new data uses will require corresponding disclosures and permission requests where applicable.
